🧠 What Does This Tool Do?

papergraph-mcp acts as a bridge between mathematical papers and AI tools. It reads papers written in LaTeX (the standard format for scientific documents) or those hosted on arXiv (a huge online archive of research papers). The tool then builds a knowledge graph — a visual network showing how different theorems, definitions, and lemmas relate to each other. This makes it much easier to:

  • 📌 See which theorems depend on others.
  • 🔗 Find connections between seemingly separate concepts.
  • 🤖 Provide AI agents with structured, usable data from raw papers.
  • 🧭 Navigate complex mathematical texts without reading every page.

Instead of manually tracking dependencies in a 50-page paper, papergraph-mcp does it automatically and saves you hours.

✨ Key Features

  • 📚 arXiv & LaTeX Support – Works directly with papers from arXiv or any LaTeX source files on your computer.

⚙️ How It Works (The Simple Version)

Think of papergraph-mcp like a high-tech librarian. When you give it a paper, here's what happens behind the scenes:

  1. Reads the Paper – It scans the LaTeX source or arXiv document.
  2. Finds the Math Parts – It identifies theorems, definitions, proofs, and equations.
  3. Maps the Links – It figures out which statements use others as building blocks.
  4. Creates the Graph – It draws a visual network where each node is a theorem and lines show dependencies.
  5. Sends to AI – It formats this graph so AI agents can directly query and reason about it.

The whole process takes just seconds for most papers. You get a bird's-eye view of the paper's mathematical structure.
